Description

Forge to Stars is a turn-based 4X grand strategy game. You begin on a single forgotten planet — an industrial society taking its first steps — and rise, era by era, until you reach for the throne of a shattered galactic empire.

It plays across two connected scales: a hex-based planet you develop and defend by hand, and a 3D galaxy of dozens of worlds where rival empires live their own lives — exploring, colonizing and going to war whether you are watching or not. Every world can be visited, fought over and claimed, hex by hex, on the ground and in orbit.

Features

  • A living galaxy. Rival empires explore, colonize, build and wage war on their own, turn after turn.
  • Real worlds, not menus. One 3D galaxy of dozens of planets — drop down to any of them and fight for it.
  • Command the battle. Turn-based tactical combat on the ground and in orbit, led through a deck of unit cards.
  • An AI that remembers. AXIOM, your enigmatic guiding intelligence, reacts to the choices you make across the whole campaign.
  • Grow through the eras. Take a single planet from its industrial age all the way to a spacefaring power.
  • Ten languages. Fully localized in ten languages at launch.

History

Forge to Studio is a small independent studio with a single obsession: strategy games that respect the player's intelligence. Forge to Stars is our first title — built in the open, system by system, with no shortcuts and no half-built mechanics.
