WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

A galaxy that lives without you

Real worlds, not menus

Every planet in the galaxy has its own 3D map. Drop down to any of them and conquer it, hex by hex — no world is off-limits.

Five great powers

Five major factions, each with its own personality, ambitions and way of waging war — exploring, colonizing and fighting on their own, turn after turn.

A galaxy full of factions

Beyond the great powers, a host of minor factions scheme, trade and fight for their place among the stars.

A world of deep lore

Every faction, world and war is rooted in a deep, written history — waiting for you to uncover it.

Command the battle

Turn-based tactical combat on the ground and in orbit, led through a deck of unit cards.

THE POWERS

Who rules the ashes

Auren Alliance

Self-proclaimed heirs of the fallen Empire — arrogant, influential and masters of legitimacy and diplomacy.

Draeth Horde

Warrior nomads who lost their frozen homeworld. They move fast, strike hard and never settle.

Kaelith Theocracy

Crystal-skinned zealots bound by faith, who worship a silent god they believe still watches the stars.

Omvex Corporation

A megacorporation where everything has a price. They negotiate even in the middle of a war.

Zethari Conclave

Transhuman technocrats always one era ahead, trading knowledge for influence.

…and a galaxy of minor powers

Beyond the five: pirate clans like the Red Claw, mercenary companies, free ports, exiled survivors and forgotten colonies — dozens of factions fighting for their place.

THE LORE

The rise and fall of the galaxy

  1. Ancient Era

    The old ones

    Long before the Empire, the galaxy's oldest ones watched the stars alone.

  2. The Founding

    One pact, one Empire

    Twenty-eight spacefaring peoples united under a single pact — the Great Galactic Empire was born.

  3. The Golden Age

    The Long Sun

    Millennia of peace, science and shared cities across thousands of worlds.

  4. The Decline

    Rot from within

    Corruption, dynastic wars and economic collapse hollowed the Empire from within.

  5. Year Zero

    The Fall

    In a single year the capital burned, the currency died and seven worlds fell silent. The Empire was over.

  6. 0–3000 after the Fall

    The Dark Years

    Three thousand years of fragmentation — successor powers, countless minor factions and a galaxy that forgot what it was.

  7. Now · 3000 PG

    A spark in the dark

    From a single forgotten planet, a new power stirs. The empire won't reunify itself.
